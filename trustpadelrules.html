Tournament Format & Player Notes

Brentwood Padel Club  ·  All players on court for 11:30am
Powered by

1 Before you start

  • Be ready and on court for 11:30am — please don't be late.
  • Round 1 opens with a five-minute warm-up, and play begins immediately afterwards.
  • Listen out for the round call, then head straight to your allocated court.

2 The pool stage

  • Six pool rounds, played across Courts 1–6.
  • Every game lasts 20 minutes.
  • Americano format — every game you win is added to your own cumulative score.
  • At deuce it's a Golden Point: sudden death, no advantage. The receiving team chooses which player receives the serve.

3 Reaching the knockout

  • After six rounds, the top 16 players on games won progress.
  • Tied on games won? The best net games (won − lost) goes through.
  • Knockout is played on Courts 5 & 6 — each game 20 minutes, winning pairs advance.
